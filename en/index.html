About me

I am a psychiatrist with subspecialty training in Consultation-Liaison Psychiatry (CMN "20 de Noviembre", ISSSTE) and a Master's degree in Cognitive-Behavioral Therapy. That means I can offer a complete medical evaluation, medication when it is needed, and evidence-based psychotherapy, all with the same doctor.
